Daily rhythm (Mon 19 to Sat 24)

  • Morning, 3.5 to 4 h: RadWise in sets of 50, review each set straight after.
  • Afternoon, 3 h: theory books, active notes, not passive reading.
  • Evening, 1 to 1.5 h: 30 to 40 questions from your own bank, then the error log.
  • Sleep at least 7 h.

Rules

  • Keep "Include seen questions" off on RadWise so you only get unseen ones.
  • If you fall more than 40 questions behind, cut own-bank time. Never cut RadWise or sleep.
  • Error log: one line per wrong or guessed question, with the learning point.
  • No new books after Friday. Revise what you already have.
  • Nuclear medicine, breast and obstetrics: skim only, they carry very few exam marks.
