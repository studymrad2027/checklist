RadWise sync bookmark

Drag this button onto your Chrome bookmarks bar (show it with Cmd+Shift+B):

Sync RadWise

  1. Open your RadWise "Browse by Category" dashboard.
  2. Click the "Sync RadWise" bookmark.
  3. You'll see "Checklist updated". Open your checklist and the finished sets are ticked.

If dragging doesn't work: make a new bookmark by hand, name it "Sync RadWise", and paste this into the URL field.
